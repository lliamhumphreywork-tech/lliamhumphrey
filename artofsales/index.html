Why setting is the way in

You don't need experience

Setting is conversations, not hard selling. You find out what someone needs and book them in with a closer.

Teams hire remotely

Businesses selling high-ticket offers need setters to book calls for their closers. Most don't care where you live.

It's a skill you keep

Once you can run a good call, it works for any offer. And it's the first step to closing.

Questions

Do I need sales experience?

No. The training starts from zero. If you can hold a normal conversation, you can learn this.

How much time does it take each week?

Plan for the weekly live session plus a few hours of practice. You can do it around your job.

Is this a job?

No. Art of Sales is training and a community. It doesn't employ you or pay you. It gets you ready to apply for remote sales roles.

Can I cancel?

Yes, anytime. You pay month to month.

What if I don't get hired?

There's no guarantee you will. Most first roles are commission-only, and it can take a while. The skills stay with you either way, and you can keep practising with us.
