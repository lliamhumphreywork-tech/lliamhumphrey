Privacy policy

Last updated 14 November 2026

Art of Sales is run by Lliam Humphrey in Johannesburg. This page explains what I collect and why, in line with South Africa's Protection of Personal Information Act (POPIA).

What I collect

When you join the waitlist: your first name and email address, plus your answers to the three optional questions (what you want sales to change for you, the most you'd pay a month, and what you earn a month). Nothing else.

Why

To email you about Art of Sales, starting with when doors open, and to shape what it includes and costs. I don't sell or share your details with anyone.

Where it's stored

Your name and email are stored in Brevo, the email service I use. Brevo stores data in the EU.

Analytics

The site uses PostHog (EU) to count visits and button clicks. It sets no cookies, doesn't record your screen, and doesn't know who you are.

Your rights

You can unsubscribe from any email with one click. You can also ask me to show you, correct or delete what I hold about you. Contact details will be added here before membership opens; until then, message @artofsales.io on Instagram.
