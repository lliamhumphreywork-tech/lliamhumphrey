Privacy policy

Last updated 18 November 2026

Art of Sales is run by Lliam Humphrey in Johannesburg. This page explains what I collect and why, in line with South Africa's Protection of Personal Information Act (POPIA).

What I collect

When you join the waitlist: your first name and email address, your answers to two questions (what feels like a fair monthly price, and what best describes you right now), and your WhatsApp number and Instagram handle if you choose to give them. Nothing else.

Why

To email you about Art of Sales, starting with a welcome email and then when doors open, and to shape what it includes and costs. If you give your WhatsApp number or Instagram handle, I'll only use them to message you about the waitlist. I don't sell or share your details with anyone.

Where it's stored

Your details and answers are stored in MailerLite, the email service I use. MailerLite stores data in the EU.

Analytics

The site uses PostHog (EU) to count visits and button clicks. It sets no cookies, doesn't record your screen, and doesn't know who you are.
