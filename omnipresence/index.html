Your list already knows you

Most firms I talk to are sitting on a list of past clients, old enquiries and people who once asked for a quote. Nobody writes to them. It's not that the firm doesn't care. The partners are busy, and email always loses to billable work.

Meanwhile, the people on that list are deciding who to call next. If you've gone quiet, it probably won't be you.

Email is the quietest way to fix that. No blasts, no discounts. Short, honest emails from you, sent on a steady rhythm, that remind people what you're good at and make it easy to say "let's talk."

What my own emails did

I run the email for a community I built, Private Victories Club. It's a small, warm list of about 250 people, so these are small numbers. They're real.

  • A three-email sequence for one group of applicants got no clicks on the first two emails and 20.6% on the third. That's about 7 clicks from 34 people, from the email that finally made a clear invitation.
  • One community email got 18 people clicking through to our WhatsApp community.
  • We split applicants into groups and send each group a different sequence, so people only get emails that fit where they are.

What I don't have yet is a firm's results. That's why the first three firms get a lower rate. In return, we track everything and I share the numbers with you.
